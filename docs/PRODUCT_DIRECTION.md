# CREOVA product direction

## Vision

Build a modern social platform that can outperform the established social platforms in capability, usability, creator opportunity, trust, performance and user control.

## Non-negotiable sequence

1. **Parity first:** map and implement the major Instagram and TikTok capabilities, including core, advanced, creator, business, messaging, commerce, safety and platform operations.
2. **Fix what is broken:** identify and address weaknesses such as opaque recommendations, weak creator support, poor controls, inconsistent feature access, fragmented workflows and limited transparency.
3. **CREOVA-native innovation:** introduce original capabilities after the parity baseline is substantially complete.

Do not jump to stage 3 to avoid the work of stage 1.

## Product principles

- Every account should have a coherent creator-capable foundation; account types add capabilities rather than making ordinary users second-class.
- Followers are not the only measure of value or relevance.
- Give users meaningful control over privacy, recommendations, notifications and their data.
- Make creation, discovery, communication and commerce work together without forcing users into one workflow.
- Design for mobile-first use, low-end devices, variable connectivity, accessibility and international audiences.
- Explain eligibility, restrictions, recommendation outcomes and monetization rules.
- Treat safety, privacy, security and reliability as product features, not later patches.

## Scope governance

The working requirements inventory is maintained separately. Each requirement must have an ID, user-visible behaviour, permissions, dependencies, failure/recovery behaviour, security/privacy considerations and acceptance tests. A feature is not complete because its screen exists.

## Honest status

Use only: NOT STARTED, BUILDING, TESTING, VERIFIED, BLOCKED. VERIFIED requires recorded evidence from the defined acceptance tests.
